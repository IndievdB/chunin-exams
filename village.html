演習場

Training Grounds

Three wooden posts, a river, and a memorial stone. Many legends began here.

Two bells. Three students. One jōnin who has no intention of losing.

The real lesson isn't how to take a bell — it's discovering that no one passes alone. Teamwork above all.

  1. Leaf concentration — hold a leaf to your forehead with chakra alone.
  2. Tree climbing — walk up a trunk using only your feet.
  3. Water walking — stand on the surface of the river.

Master all three before attempting advanced jutsu.

Not every ninja can mold ninjutsu — but anyone can train. A daily regimen from the hall of hard work:

  • 500 laps around the village
  • 1,000 push-ups (on fingertips)
  • 1,000 squats with weights
  • Kick the training post until sunset

If you can't finish, double it tomorrow!

中忍選抜試験

The Chūnin Exams

Genin from every allied village gather to test their strength, intelligence, and resolve.

  1. 一

    The Written Test

    Ten questions. Proctors on every row. Get caught cheating clumsily three times and your whole team fails — but the true test is the tenth question.

  2. 二

    The Forest of Death

    Training Ground 44. Five days to secure both a Heaven and Earth scroll and reach the central tower. Don't open the scrolls early.

  3. 三

    The Final Tournament

    One-on-one matches before the village, its lords, and the Kage. Victory isn't required — showing you can lead is.

Practice Exam — Question 10

Before you see the final question, you must decide: take it, and if you answer wrong you remain genin forever. Or walk away now, and your team fails this year.

The Noble Blue Beast

Might Guy — taijutsu master, eternal rival, and the loudest believer in the Springtime of Youth.

A jōnin of the Hidden Leaf and leader of Team Guy. Unable to excel at ninjutsu or genjutsu, he poured everything into taijutsu — and became one of the strongest fighters the village has ever produced.

He's known for his green jumpsuit, his blinding smile, and a rivalry he counts in wins and losses with obsessive care.

  1. Gate of Opening
  2. Gate of Healing
  3. Gate of Life
  4. Gate of Pain
  5. Gate of Limit
  6. Gate of View
  7. Gate of Wonder
  8. Gate of Death

Each gate removes a limiter on the body's chakra flow. Every gate opened is power gained — at a terrible price.

  • Hard work beats natural talent.
  • If you fail a goal, set a harder penalty and try again.
  • A rival is a gift.
  • Protect your students with your life.
